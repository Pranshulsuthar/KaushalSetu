Our approach

Why we are building this

Skills are built on the job. Recognition should follow.

Millions of workers learn through doing — fixing a fault, shaping wood, installing a panel or solving a customer's problem. Much of that skill is real, but hard to show.

KaushalSetu is a concept for an AI-assisted, evidence-based platform that helps workers turn everyday work into a clear, portable skill profile — while keeping the worker in control.

  • Evidence over assumptions

    Work is documented through voice, text and photos — not only certificates.

  • Worker-first privacy

    People decide what to share, and with whom.

  • Language-inclusive

    Designed for Hindi, English and future Indian languages.

  • Portable by design

    A work identity that can travel across employers and locations.

What “evidence-based” means here

KaushalSetu helps organise work records into probable skills. It does not replace formal certification or claim government validation.

“The best proof of skill is the work itself.”

Our design principle

Your work, in your pocket

Meet KaushalPass

A simple, shareable view of a worker's skills, evidence and learning journey — designed to travel across employers, trainers and locations.

  • One profile for skills, work evidence and learning progress.
  • A simple QR view for sharing with employers or trainers — with consent.
  • Clear labels that separate self-reported work from verified evidence.
  • Available in Hindi and English.

KaushalPass is a private, worker-controlled profile. It is not a government-issued identity document.
